# Logo and photo sources

Retrieved October 27, 2026.

**Photo rule:** Heavenly Angels serves adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities and serious mental illness. Photos should show adults with disabilities living everyday life, not seniors or nursing-home scenes. The current site's four nurse photos all show seniors, so they're not used.

None of these photos show Heavenly Angels' actual residents, staff, or home. Replace them with permission-cleared photos of the real home and team when available.
