# Dove mascot assets

All poses come from the approved concept sheet (`design/characters/dove-concept-sheet-v1.png` in the client workspace): ivory clay body, navy oval eyes, peach beak and feet, sky blue neck scarf.

- `public/assets/dove/welcome.webp`: standing, one wing raised. AI-generated cutout from the concept sheet.
- `public/assets/dove/flight.webp`: mid-flight, both wings spread. AI-generated cutout from the concept sheet.
- `public/assets/dove/guide.webp`: the "Guide" pose, keyed out of the concept sheet's blue panel and upscaled 2x.

The dove represents the brand, never residents. More poses (reassure, thank-you, sleeping, carrying a tray) need to be generated as clean transparent cutouts; the cream-on-cream poses on the sheet can't be keyed out cleanly. A rigged Rive version is planned for wing and blink animation.
